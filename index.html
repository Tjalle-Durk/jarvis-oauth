Jarvis (personal)

A private, single-user assistant. Not offered to the public.

Jarvis is a voice assistant that runs on its owner's own laptop. It helps one person plan their day by reading their own email, calendars and selected documents, and by creating email drafts when explicitly asked. It never sends email.

The app is used only by its developer, with their own Google accounts. It is not available for sign-up and is not distributed.
