Privacy policy

Jarvis (personal) · last updated 9 November 2026

Who uses this app

Jarvis is a personal tool used only by its developer, with their own Google accounts. It is not offered to other users.

What Google data it accesses

  • Gmail (read-only, plus creating drafts): to summarise which emails need attention and, on explicit request, to prepare a draft reply. Jarvis never sends email.
  • Google Calendar (read-only): to know when the user is free or busy.
  • Google Drive (read-only, limited to folders the user selects): to answer questions about meeting notes and plans.

Where the data goes

  • All Google data is processed locally on the user's own laptop and stored in an encrypted directory there. It is not uploaded to any server operated by the developer.
  • For planning, Jarvis may send anonymised, structured summaries (for example "a board meeting from 19:30 to 21:30", with names replaced by role aliases) to the Anthropic API. Raw email bodies, documents and personal identifiers are never sent. Every such request is logged locally for the user to inspect.
  • Google data is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

Retention and deletion

Cached email bodies are deleted after 14 days; only metadata and short summaries are kept. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and deleting the app's local data directory removes everything it stored.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Jarvis's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
